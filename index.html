Welcome to NASA SAR PROJECT

Your gateway to understanding and monitoring wildfires in the Amazon.

About the Project

This project utilizes Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) technology to detect and analyze wildfire hotspots in the Amazon rainforest. Our goal is to provide accurate and timely data to help combat deforestation and protect this vital ecosystem.

Data Sources

We leverage data from various satellite missions, including Sentinel-1 and RADARSAT, to monitor changes in the Amazon region. Our data is processed and analyzed to identify potential wildfire hotspots.
